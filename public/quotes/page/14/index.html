[ENG] Ancient Recipe for Happiness (Receta antigua para la felicidad) Take 12 months, brush them clean of bitterness, avarice, pedantry, and fear, and divide each month into 30 or 31 parts, so that the supply lasts exactly for a year. It’s split up every day: 1 part work and 2 parts cheerfulness and humour. Add 3 heaping tablespoons of optimism, 1 teaspoon of tolerance, 1 grain of irony, and 1 prize tact. Then the I I mass is very generously poured with love. Decorate the finished dish with bouquets of small gifts and serve it cheerfully every day with a good, refreshing cup of tea…
